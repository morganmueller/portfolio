Product Development Lead, NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene

Data visualization engineer: front-end, D3, and design systems for public data products.

M.S. Human–Computer Interaction, NYU  ·  Adjunct Professor, Parsons School of Design  ·  New York City

I design and build data visualization products: public dashboards, decision-support tools, and the visualization systems behind them. I work end to end, from data modeling and chart architecture to interaction design, accessibility, and the React front-end that ships.

It is high-stakes work: public health data that policymakers, researchers, and journalists rely on, often during active outbreaks, built under real deadlines and public scrutiny. I also teach data visualization and computational design at Parsons.

How I work

I like to hold the whole thread, from the data to the visualization to the implementation, rather than hand it off at each seam. In practice that means prototyping early, designing the data model and the interface together, and treating the codebase as a product surface that has to last. The result is data products that ship faster and hold up longer.
